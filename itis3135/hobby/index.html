My Photography Hobby

What ✦ Who ✦ When ✦ Where ✦ How ✦ Why ✦ AI Prompts

What

I enjoy photography because it helps me notice details that I usually miss. I like taking pictures of places, small objects, and moments that feel special.

My favorite style is simple and clean photos. I try to focus on lighting and the main subject instead of adding too many things.

Who

My name is Mayar, and I like photography as a creative break from coding and studying.

I enjoy taking pictures when I travel or when I walk around campus and Charlotte.

When

The best time for photography is usually “golden hour,” right after sunrise or before sunset.

Cloudy days can also be great because the lighting is softer and shadows are not harsh.

Where

I like taking photos in parks, on campus, and in city areas with interesting buildings.

Sometimes the best spots are simple places like a coffee shop, a walkway, or a quiet street.

How

I start by choosing a subject, then I try different angles and distance.

After that, I do small edits like cropping, adjusting brightness, and making colors look natural.

Why

Photography helps me keep memories, especially when I travel or experience something new.

It also makes me feel proud when I capture a photo that looks clean and professional.
